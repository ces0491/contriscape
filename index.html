    Guide

    The city

    Each tower is one day and each block is a year. Height is the square root of that day's contributions, scaled to the user's busiest day, so ordinary days stay visible beside a huge one. Red lights mark the busiest days.

    Ways to fly

    Autopilot
    Replays the history as a flight. The city rises as the date passes. The ship flies above most days, so the busiest rise past it.
    Pilot
    You fly. Aim at a tower to read its date and count. A tap of a steering key nudges the nose and holding it turns harder. The ship stops while a panel is open.
    Orbit
    Rotate and zoom around the finished city. Point at a tower to read it.

    Buttons

    Visit
    Opens the city of the username in the box.
    Copy link
    Copies the address of this city. Larger screens only.
    Pause, Play, Replay
    Stops or resumes Autopilot. From Pilot or Orbit, Replay starts the flight again.
    All years
    Lists the years. Pick one to fly it, or All for the whole history.
    Timeline
    Drag it to move through the dates.
    Badges
    Lists the milestones in this history. Pick one to fly to it. The ship waits there while you line up the shot, and Shift flies off.
    Settings
    Sound, mouse steering, Invert Y, and the colours of the buildings and ship. Sound starts at your first click, tap or key press, because browsers hold it back until then, and an iPhone in silent mode keeps it quiet. With mouse steering on, the ship turns towards the cursor. It's off by default, so the mouse stays free for the buttons.
    Your year on GitHub
    A December flight through the current year, with captions and a summary.

    Why these badges

    These are contriscape's own badges, worked out from dates and counts in GitHub's public data, such as a first pull request, a longest streak or a busiest day. The achievements on GitHub profiles aren't shown because GitHub has no API for them, its policy only allows scraping for research and archiving, and the artwork is GitHub's.

    Shooting a badge blows it up and saves it in this browser. It changes nothing on GitHub. Reset, at the top of the Badges list, brings back the ones you've earned on this city so you can shoot them again.

    Your data

    A city shows what a public GitHub profile already shows. This site has no accounts, cookies or analytics. Your badges and settings stay in this browser. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
